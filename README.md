## 🧠 The 4-Step Overwhelm Reset Method

### Step 1: Dump It (The 5-Minute Brain Dump & Priority Sorter)
Stop trying to hold 20 swirling reminders in your working memory. Empty every task, worry, and errand onto paper, then sort them into **4 Calm Boxes**:
* **Box 1 — Do Today (Max 3 Items):** Only items that truly cannot wait until tomorrow.
* **Box 2 — Shrink It (Too Big):** Projects that freeze you because they secretly contain 10 hidden steps.
* **Box 3 — Park for Later:** Good ideas and non-urgent errands safe on paper for next week.
* **Box 4 — Let It Go (Zero Guilt):** Low-value expectations you give yourself permission to drop.

### Step 2: Pick It (Today's Top 3 Focus Page)
Ditch the 30-item to-do list that makes you feel behind before 9:00 AM. Circle just **1 to 3 priorities** that will make today feel like a win.

### Step 3: Shrink It (The 2-Minute Task Shrinker)
Take the 1 scary task you have been avoiding and break it into **5 tiny micro-steps**:
* Make **Micro-Step #1** so small it takes less than **120 seconds** (e.g., *"Open the blank document and type the title"* or *"Put 3 dishes in the sink"*).

### Step 4: Start for 2 Minutes (Momentum Timer)
Run a 2-minute timer. You only have to complete Micro-Step #1. Once you cross the starting line, natural momentum takes over.
