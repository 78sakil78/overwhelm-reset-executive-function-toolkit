## 🧠 Why Traditional Planners Fail When Your Mind Is Full

When working memory is overloaded with 25 swirling reminders, traditional hourly schedules trigger **task paralysis** and **all-or-nothing perfectionism**.

**The Overwhelm Reset Method** replaces rigid schedules with four gentle, dopamine-friendly steps:

1. **STEP 1 · DUMP IT (Clear Working Memory):** Empty every task, errand, and worry out of your head onto paper so your brain stops trying to hold them all at once.
2. **STEP 2 · PICK IT (Priority Sorter):** Sort the clutter into four calm boxes (*Do Today, Shrink It, Park for Later, Let It Go*) and circle **just ONE main priority** for today.
3. **STEP 3 · SHRINK IT (The 2-Minute Task Shrinker):** Break that one scary task into 5 tiny micro-steps. Make Step 1 so small it takes less than 120 seconds.
4. **STEP 4 · START FOR 2 MINUTES:** Run a 2-minute timer and do only the first micro-step. Once you cross the starting line, momentum does the rest.
